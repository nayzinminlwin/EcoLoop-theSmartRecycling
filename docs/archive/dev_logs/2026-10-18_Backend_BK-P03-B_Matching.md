## Implementation notes

- Reconciliation uses deterministic pair scoring with IoU, center-distance and area-ratio checks, and prevents more than one candidate from the same model entering a group.
- Conflicting specific labels are grouped when geometry supports one physical object, then become an explicit unresolved decision rather than a raw-score majority vote.
- Generic `can` and `glass` labels remain unresolved; cardboard is recognized but excluded; unsupported and false-detection labels remain traceable.
- Attached bottle caps may join the bottle candidate; loose caps remain separate candidates.
- Count aggregation consumes resolved object records once and excludes tested false detections.
